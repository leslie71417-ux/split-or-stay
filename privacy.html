Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

Split or Stay is an anonymous service where people post relationship dilemmas and others vote on them. It is run by an individual operator based in Tokyo, Japan. Contact: .

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. When you first open the app, we create a random ID for your browser. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • What you post. Your question text, the tags and relationship length you choose, the audience you pick (Japan, US or Global), and any follow-up you add.
  • Your votes and comments, together with the region you selected (US, Japan or Other).
  • Basic activity. When you first and last used the app, and the language you use, so we can count active users.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers process your IP address and browser information to deliver the service and prevent abuse.

We do not ask for your real name. Please do not include real names, workplaces, schools, social media handles or other details that could identify you or anyone else. The app automatically hides email addresses, phone numbers, links and @handles, but cannot catch everything.

What other people can see

Questions, follow-ups, vote totals by region and comments are public to everyone using the app. Nobody else can see which questions you posted or how you voted. Your anonymous ID is never shown.

How we use it

To run the app, show results, count usage, keep the service safe (rate limits, reports, removing content that breaks our Terms), and improve the product. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted by Supabase in Tokyo, Japan. The website is delivered by Vercel, a US company, through a global network. This means your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

Third-party services this site connects to

  • Supabase (database and anonymous sign-in) — your anonymous ID, posts, votes and IP address.
  • Vercel (hosting) — IP address and browser information.
  • Google Fonts (fonts) and jsDelivr (code library) — your browser downloads files from them, which shares your IP address and browser information.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we close the service. Content removed for breaking our rules is deleted from the app.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us to delete your posts, votes and comments, or ask what we hold. Because accounts are anonymous, email us at with the question text (or a screenshot) so we can find it. If someone wrote about you in a way you can recognize, you can ask us to remove it the same way. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Clearing your browser data deletes your anonymous ID from your device. After that, we can no longer link you to your earlier posts automatically.

Age

Split or Stay is only for people aged 18 or older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

利用目的

データベースは Supabase 社の東京リージョンに保存されます。ウェブサイトは米国の Vercel 社のグローバルネットワークを通じて配信されるため、アメリカを含む外国で情報が処理される場合があります。

外部送信について

本サイトは、以下の事業者のサービスに接続します。

開示・削除のご依頼

ご自身の投稿・投票・コメントの削除や、保有している情報の開示を依頼できます。アカウントが匿名のため、該当する相談の本文（またはスクリーンショット）を添えて までご連絡ください。ご自身のことが書かれていると分かる投稿の削除も、同じ方法でご依頼いただけます。原則7日以内に対応します。

ブラウザのデータを消去すると、端末から匿名IDが削除され、以前の投稿と自動的に紐づけることはできなくなります。
